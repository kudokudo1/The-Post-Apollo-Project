This release introduces a small historical PRIMARY-SOURCE catalogue: Heinrich Cornelius Agrippa, Three Books of Occult Philosophy, Book I, chapters XXIII–XXIX, using the 1651 English edition. It is explicitly ONE named text/edition and contains representative lists, not a universal list of spiritual, medical, or physical facts. Every planet row carries its original chapter number:

- Sun: chapter XXIII (gold, chrysolite, marigold)
- Moon: chapter XXIV (silver, selenite, rosemary)
- Saturn: chapter XXV (lead, gold by weight, onyx, rue)
- Jupiter: chapter XXVI (tin, silver, gold, sapphire, basil)
- Mars: chapter XXVII (iron, red brass, bloodstone, garlic)
- Venus: chapter XXVIII (silver and yellow/red brass, emerald, vervain)
- Mercury: chapter XXIX (quicksilver, tin, agate, hazel)

Historically attested overlaps are preserved, notably silver for Moon/Jupiter/Venus, sapphire for Saturn/Jupiter/Venus and red brass for Mars/Venus. Quicksilver is a historical label only; the UI gives no physical handling instructions. The text offers a wider, sometimes contradictory network of associations, so M6e does not imply these examples are exhaustive or exclusive. Post-Apollo palette colors are purely visual UI inks and are NOT claimed to come from Agrippa.

## HOW TO USE

After approved GitHub merge and separately authorized Fedora installation: Weather Station > MAGICK > LENSES (or INDEX > PLANETARY MAGICK > CORRESPONDENCES). The familiar landing appears first. Select AGRIPPA to show the planetary attribution wheel. The seven sectors identify historical planetary categories; its three bands represent metals, stones, plants. Select a segment to read its modernized historical terms and exact chapter. Select MATRIX for a 7×3 at-a-glance table with the same clickable entries. BODY arrows or scroll move among the seven planets. BACK returns to the original LENSES landing. No internet/browser activity is required in the widget.

## WATCH OUT FOR

This is one specific historical author/edition. Other Hermetic, astrological, religious, folk or modern traditions do not necessarily agree, and multiple planetary associations apply to a single metal/stone/herb. Historical attributions are not evidence of medical effects, physical planetary forces, guaranteed results or safe handling of substances. Metallic mercury, toxic herbs and rituals are NOT promoted or instructed. The small visual color palette is UI styling, not a sourced color correspondence chart.

M6d was only verified merged, not operator-native accepted in this chat. Do not assume success for M6e on desktop from CI alone.
