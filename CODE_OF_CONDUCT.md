# CODE OF CONDUCT // META APOLLO LOGOS // POST-APOLLO FAMILY

The **Meta Apollo Logos // Post-Apollo Family** is built around relationships.

That includes the relationships between people.

Community does not require everyone to agree, speak the same way, think the same way, or approach a problem from the same direction. Disagreement, criticism, argument, experimentation, and correction are all normal parts of building something together.

What matters is whether those interactions remain in **good faith**.

## GOOD FAITH

Good faith means treating the other people in the community as people rather than obstacles, targets, resources, or abstractions.

You can strongly disagree with an idea.

You can say that a design is bad.

You can criticize an implementation, challenge an assumption, reject a proposal, question a model, or argue that something should be rebuilt entirely.

None of that requires attacking the person who brought it forward.

Criticism should remain connected to the work, evidence, reasoning, behavior, or decision being criticized.

## RELATIONSHIPS OF TRUST

Participation creates an expected relationship of trust between community members.

That does not mean blind trust.

It means people should reasonably expect that participation will not be used as an excuse for harassment, intimidation, humiliation, stalking, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate exposure of private information, sustained personal attacks, or other behavior intended to make participation unsafe.

Do not use access to the community, its projects, or its people to exploit information that was shared in confidence.

Do not impersonate another person or intentionally misrepresent someone else's words, work, identity, or intentions.

Do not organize harassment or encourage other people to target someone.

## DISAGREEMENT IS ALLOWED

Being uncomfortable is not automatically the same thing as being mistreated.

Someone disagreeing with you is not harassment.

Someone rejecting your contribution is not harassment.

Someone asking you to support a claim with evidence is not harassment.

Someone criticizing your work is not harassment.

At the same time, repeatedly using "criticism" as a cover for hostility, humiliation, intimidation, or personal targeting does not become acceptable merely because the conversation began with a technical disagreement.

Context matters.

Pattern matters.

Good faith matters.

## CONTRIBUTION DOES NOT BUY AUTHORITY

Contributing code, documentation, money, testing, research, artwork, attention, or time does not give someone ownership over another person or automatic authority over the project.

Likewise, maintainers should not treat contributors as disposable labor.

Contribution creates a relationship. It does not erase anyone's agency.

## CORRECTION

People will make mistakes.

They will misunderstand one another, communicate badly, make incorrect claims, become frustrated, or cross a boundary without realizing it.

Whenever reasonable, the first goal should be correction rather than punishment.

Explain the problem.

Clarify the boundary.

Give people an opportunity to adjust.

A community that cannot tolerate correction cannot learn.

But correction also requires participation from the person being corrected. Repeatedly ignoring clear boundaries, continuing harmful behavior after being asked to stop, or treating every attempt at correction as an invitation to escalate may require stronger action.

## MODERATION

Maintainers may remove content, close conversations, reject contributions, restrict participation, or remove someone from a project space when necessary to protect the community or the relationships of trust within it.

Those decisions should be based on behavior and context rather than personal dislike or disagreement alone.

Severe behavior may justify immediate action without first going through an extended warning process.

When practical, moderation should aim to explain what relationship was violated and what would need to change for that relationship to be repaired.

## SCOPE

This code applies to participation in official Meta Apollo Logos // Post-Apollo project spaces and to interactions that directly affect those spaces or the safety of people participating in them.

It is not an attempt to govern someone's entire life or every disagreement they have elsewhere.

## THE INTENT

The goal is not politeness for its own sake.

The goal is a community where people can disagree, experiment, criticize, fail, correct one another, contribute, and build relationships without having to sacrifice their dignity or agency to participate.

**Good faith is expected.**

**Disagreement is allowed.**

**Criticism is allowed.**

**Correction is part of the process.**

**Relationships of trust should be protected.**
