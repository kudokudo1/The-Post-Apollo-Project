# CONTRIBUTING // POST-APOLLO

Contributions are welcome when they help the project without erasing the relationships, provenance, and design intent that make the project what it is.

## Before contributing

- Read the repository README and any local architecture or design notes.
- Keep changes focused enough to understand and review.
- Preserve existing provenance, license notices, and third-party attribution.
- Do not silently replace project-specific behavior with a generic equivalent merely because the generic version is easier.
- If a change affects a major ownership boundary, architecture rule, or user-facing relationship, explain why.

Issues, questions, evidence, bug reports, documentation corrections, and experiments are useful contributions too.

## Contributor rights

You must have the legal right to submit what you contribute.

Do not submit third-party code, text, artwork, data, or other material unless its license permits inclusion here and you clearly identify its origin and license.

## Licensing of accepted contributions

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, accepted contributions become part of the same licensing structure that applies to the surrounding project material.

Third-party or inherited material remains governed by its own license.

## Review and acceptance

Submitting something does not guarantee that it will be merged.

A contribution may be declined because of architecture, maintenance cost, project direction, provenance concerns, licensing concerns, duplication, quality, timing, or simply because it does not belong in this project.

That is not a judgment about the contributor.

It is maintenance.
